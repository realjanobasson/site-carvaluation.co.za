Privacy notice

This notice explains how Car Valuation collects and uses information submitted through carvaluation.co.za.

Information we collect

We collect the details you choose to submit, such as your name, email, phone, company or dealership, vehicle details and enquiry message. We also retain the source domain, page, referral and campaign information needed to manage the enquiry.

Why we use it

We use this information to respond to your request, route it to the appropriate DealershipCore workflow, maintain an audit trail and improve the customer journey. We do not use the enquiry form to make an automated credit decision.

Sharing and storage

Enquiries are stored in the DealershipCore management environment and may be shared only with the team or approved service provider needed to respond. We do not sell submitted contact details.

Your choices

You may ask to access, correct or delete personal information, subject to legal and operational retention requirements. Send the request through the website enquiry form and identify it as a privacy request.

Security and retention

Reasonable technical and organisational safeguards are used. Information is retained only for as long as needed for the enquiry, accountability and applicable legal requirements.

Contact

Use the enquiry form on carvaluation.co.za for questions about this notice.
