Useful answers first

Know what the journey does—and what it does not promise.

Is an online valuation a guaranteed offer?+

No. It is an informed estimate. A final offer can depend on inspection, history, condition and buyer requirements.

What information should I have ready?+

Make, model, year, mileage, variant, service history and a fair description of condition.

Why can two valuations differ?+

Sources may use different assumptions, time periods, locations and definitions of retail, trade and purchase value.

How is my information used?+

It is used to manage and respond to the valuation request under the privacy notice and recorded consent.
