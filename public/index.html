Useful answers first

Questions worth answering before you enquire.

Is an online car valuation a guaranteed offer?+

No. It is an informed estimate. A final offer depends on physical inspection, history, condition and buyer requirements.

What information should I have ready?+

Make, model, year, mileage, variant where known, service history and a fair description of condition.

Where does my request go?+

It enters the connected Vehicle Valuation and DealershipCore enquiry pipeline with the source recorded as carvaluation.co.za.

Do you sell my contact details?+

No. Details are used to respond to the request under the privacy notice and recorded consent.
